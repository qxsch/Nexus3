NEXUS·3

Three-deck DJ console for the browser

  • AudioWorklet turntables: sample-accurate varispeed, reverse & real scratching
  • Worker-thread analysis: BPM detection, beat grid and multi-band waveforms
  • Auto-sync: tempo match plus a phase-locked loop that keeps decks glued
  • Split output: master to the speakers, PFL cue to a second device or channels 3/4
  • Indexed crate: SQLite full-text search over your local folders, plus Jamendo

Audio needs a user gesture to start. Chromium browsers give the best results (setSinkId for headphone cueing).
